Baccarat Odds & House Edge

Every figure below was computed by enumerating all 1,659,001 reachable coups of a fresh 8-deck shoe, each weighted by its exact probability. No simulation, no rounding up from someone else's table — and you can re-run it yourself.

How often each side wins

ResultProbability
Banker wins45.859742%
Player wins44.624661%
Tie9.515597%

Those three sum to 100% and match the canonical 8-deck figures to six decimal places, which is the check that the drawing tableau behind them is correct.

House edge on the main bets

BetPaysHouse edge
Banker0.95 : 1 (5% commission)1.06%

Each falls straight out of the probabilities above. Banker: 0.45859742 × 0.95 − 0.44624661 = −0.0106. Player: 0.44624661 − 0.45859742 = −0.0124. Tie: 0.09515597 × 8 − 0.90484403 = −0.1436.

Banker is the best bet on the table, commission included. The Tie costs roughly twelve times as much per unit staked.

A Tiger Tie at 35:1 and one at 45:1 are the same wager with nearly triple the cost. Two circles printed with the same words are not the same bet.

Check the numbers yourself

The engine is open source, and the enumeration is a test you can run in about three seconds:

cargo test -p baccarat-engine --test exact_enumeration -- --nocapture

It walks every reachable coup, asserts the leaf probabilities sum to 1, and prints the table above.
